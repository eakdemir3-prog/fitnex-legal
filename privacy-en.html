Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Developer: EA Vision Labs · Package: com.eavisionlabs.fitnex · Türkçe

FITNEX is a personal fitness app for tracking workouts and nutrition. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it is stored and what rights you have over it. In short: your data is stored on your phone and in a cloud backup linked to your account that only you can access. The backup exists to bring your data back when you change phones or reinstall the app. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

How is the cloud backup protected? The backup is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore, on Google servers in the United States. This transfer happens only with your explicit consent, given at sign-up or later in the app; if you don't consent, your data stays only on your phone. You can withdraw your consent anytime in Profile › Backup, and your cloud backup will then be deleted. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Security rules ensure each user can access only their own account's backup; other users cannot see your data. The backup is used only to restore your data to you and is not processed for analysis, advertising or any other purpose.

The app has no ads, no advertising ID and no location tracking; usage statistics are used only to improve the app, not for marketing or advertising. For app security, device and app integrity are verified with Google Play Integrity (Firebase App Check). The camera is used only when you scan a barcode (Google's barcode scanner) or take a progress photo; barcode images are not saved, and progress photos are stored only on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Payments

Subscription payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details. Our subscription service provider RevenueCat is used to verify whether your subscription is active. Your email address is not sent to RevenueCat; only an anonymous, irreversible ID derived from your email and your purchase record (which plan, start and end date) are shared.

Service providers we share data with

We do not sell or rent your data or share it with anyone for marketing. Only the following services are used to run the app:

  • Google: Google Sign-In and Google Play Billing. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering app announcements to your phone.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: subscription verification. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics and App Check: sign-in, password reset emails, cloud backup, anonymous usage statistics and security verification.
  • Health Connect (Android): only with your permission, step count, weight and body fat are read; if you also turn it on, your workouts' active calories are written.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: app crash and error reports. Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Open Food Facts (an open, non-profit food database): product information for scanned barcodes. Open Food Facts Privacy Policy

Free trial and cancelling a subscription

New users can try the subscription plans free for 3 days through Google Play. The trial can be used once per Google account. If you cancel in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions before the trial ends, you won't be charged; otherwise the price of your chosen plan is charged automatically. You can cancel your subscription anytime in the same place; features stay unlocked until the end of the period you paid for.

Notifications

Workout, water and challenge reminders are scheduled locally by your phone's own notification system. General announcements, such as new features and offers, may also be sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging; for this, your phone is assigned a random notification ID. These announcements are the same for everyone, and your personal health data is not used for them. You can turn notifications off anytime in your phone settings.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept on your phone and in the cloud backup until you delete your account. In the app, go to Profile › System › Delete My Account to permanently delete your account, cloud backup and all your data; the cloud backup is deleted immediately. Reset All Data also deletes the cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes only the data on your phone; your cloud backup stays in your account and comes back when you sign in again. For step-by-step instructions and deletion of your RevenueCat record, see the Account Deletion page.

Health information notice

FITNEX's suggestions and Smart Coach replies are for general information only and are not medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional before starting a new exercise or nutrition program, especially if you have a health condition.

Children

FITNEX is not intended for people under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from people under that age.

Your rights

Under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right to request information about your data and to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. You can view, edit and delete your data directly in the app. For other requests, write to us and we'll reply within 30 days.
