Delete your account and data

FITNEX · Developer: EA Vision Labs · Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Türkçe

You can permanently delete your FITNEX account and all data linked to it at any time. The fastest way is from inside the app. If you can't access the app, you can also send a request by email.

Delete in the app

  1. Open the FITNEX app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to the Profile screen.
  3. In the System section at the bottom, tap Delete My Account.
  4. Read the warning and confirm with Permanently Delete My Account.

Deletion happens immediately and can't be undone. The data on your phone and your cloud backup are deleted together. Simply uninstalling the app does not delete your account; your cloud backup stays in your account so you can sign in again.

If you have a subscription: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription. To stop being charged, first follow Google Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › FITNEX › Cancel subscription.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, or you also want your subscription record deleted, send us an email titled "FITNEX account deletion" from the email address you used for your account. We'll complete your request within 30 days and let you know by email.

eavisionlabs@gmail.com

What may be kept

Purchase and billing records are kept by Google Play and are subject to Google's own retention periods for legal obligations (tax and accounting). FITNEX cannot delete these records. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Nutrition values you entered for a barcoded product and contributed to the shared product database (product name and values) remain anonymous; this record does not contain your name or email. The record of an invite code you used can be deleted along with your account on request.
